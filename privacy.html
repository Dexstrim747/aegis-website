Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for the Telegram bot Aegis

This policy explains what information the Aegis Telegram bot ("Aegis", "the bot") processes when you or your community use it, and how that information is handled.

Data we collect

  • Basic Telegram identifiers needed to operate: user ID, username, and chat/group ID.
  • Administrative settings you configure for your community (rules, permissions, preferences).
  • Interaction data required to process commands and moderation actions, such as message metadata used for spam and abuse detection.

Aegis does not collect data beyond what is required for the bot to function.

How we use data

  • To operate administrative and moderation features you enable.
  • To apply the configuration set by a group's administrators.
  • To maintain reliability, including monitoring and diagnosing failures.

Data is not sold, and is not used for advertising.

Data storage and security

Data is stored only for as long as it's needed to provide the bot's functionality. Access to stored data is limited to what's required for the platform's operation, and administrative actions taken through the bot are scoped to the permissions granted to it.

Third parties

Aegis operates through the Telegram Bot API. Telegram's own privacy policy governs data processed by Telegram itself. Aegis does not share collected data with third parties beyond what is required to operate the service.

Your choices

Group administrators can remove Aegis from a community at any time, which stops further data collection for that community. Individual users can stop interacting with the bot at any point.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the project's source can be directed through the project repository.
